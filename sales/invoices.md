# Invoices (Square)

Set these up once as **Items** in Square, then any deposit invoice takes about 30 seconds to send.

| Item name | Price | When to send |
|---|---|---|
| Website Design & Launch, Standard (50% deposit) | $450 | The moment they say yes to Standard |
| Website Design & Launch, Standard (balance) | $450 | On launch day |
| Website Design & Launch, Starter (50% deposit) | $250 | The moment they say yes to Starter |
| Website Design & Launch, Starter (balance) | $250 | On launch day |
| Website Care Plan (monthly) | $39 | Start on launch day as a **recurring** monthly invoice |

**Invoice settings:** Due upon receipt. Allow both card and bank transfer (ACH).
Fees on a $450 deposit are about $15.15 by card and $4.50 by bank transfer, so mention "bank transfer is easiest" when you send it.

**Invoice message (copy and paste):**
> Thanks for choosing us for your new website! This 50% deposit reserves your spot and gets work started. Your site will be live within about a week of receiving your logo, photos and business details. The remaining balance is due when the site launches. Questions? Just reply to this email or call me at ___.

**When they say yes on the phone:**
1. "Great. What's the best email for the invoice?"
2. Send the deposit invoice from the Square app while you're still on the call.
3. "You'll see it in your inbox now. Once that's taken care of, I'll send a short list of what I need from you."
4. Tell me they said yes, and I'll prep their launch (`sales/launch-checklist.md`).
