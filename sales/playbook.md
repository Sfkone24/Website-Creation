# Sales playbook: first 4 HVAC leads (Bloomington, IN)

Goal: **close 1 of 4 this week** = $900 one-time + $39/mo recurring. Then repeat the process for the next 10.

## Why now
It's October, the start of heating season. "Furnace repair near me" searches climb through November to January. Every week without a site is calls going to competitors. Use that urgency, honestly.

## Week plan
| Day | Do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Turn on the demo host (see root README) and open every demo link on your phone. Call all 4 leads (Tue–Thu, 10–11:30am or 1:30–3pm). Leave voicemails and send Facebook page messages with the mobile screenshot. |
| 2–3 | Walk in at Allen's (1015 N Curry Pike) and Harris (115 W Grimes Ln) with the demo on your phone. |
| 4–5 | Follow up once with anyone who saw it. Offer to tweak colors, wording or photos. |
| 7 | Final note: "I'll take the demo down Friday unless you'd like it." |

Log every touch in `prospecting/prospects.csv` (`status`, `last_contact`).

## The 60-second in-person demo
1. Open their demo on your phone and hand it to them.
2. "Tap the call button." It dials their own number.
3. Scroll to the services: "These are your services, in plain English."
4. Scroll to the request form: "Customers can request service at 10pm, and it lands in your inbox."
5. "I can have this live on your own domain within a week. It's $900, or $500 for the basic version."
6. Then stop talking.

## Objections
- **"I get all my work from word of mouth."** "That's great. Referrals still Google you before they call. This makes sure they find the right number."
- **"Too expensive."** "One furnace install pays for this several times over. There's also the $500 version."
- **"I already have a site."** (Harris) Show both side by side on the phone and point out the "Not Secure" warning.
- **"Let me think about it."** "Sure. What would you want changed on it?" Then make the change. A tweaked demo is a better follow-up than a nudge.

## Closing
- Send the proposal (`sales/proposal-template.md`) and a 50% deposit invoice (Square, Stripe or PayPal invoices all work).
- Once the deposit lands, run `sales/launch-checklist.md`.
- Set up the $39/mo Care Plan as a recurring invoice on launch day.

## Rules
- Only real facts on the sites. Anything marked "confirm" in the lead file gets confirmed before launch.
- No cold text messages (TCPA). Call, message their Facebook page, visit, or email. Honor any "no thanks" immediately.
- Demos stay labeled "Concept preview" and `noindex` until the client pays and approves.
