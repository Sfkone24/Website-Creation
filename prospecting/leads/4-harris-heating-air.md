## Why they're a prospect
They already have a site, so they believe websites matter. But theirs works against them:
- **It's HTTP only**, so Chrome and Safari show **"Not Secure"** on every page (http://harrisheatingandair.com).
- **It's a dated multi-page build** with pages named `index_003.htm`, `index_014.htm`, `index_022.htm` and so on.
- **It contradicts itself.** Some pages say "Since 1982" and others say "Since 1988." The contact page lists 3908 S. Walnut St Suite A while directories say 115 W Grimes Ln. The homepage shows two phone numbers.
- **It hides their selling points:** ClimateMaster geothermal, 95% efficient Tempstar furnaces, commercial units from 5 to 20 tons, free estimates on new equipment, and repairs on all makes and models.

## Pitch angle
> "Your site shows a 'Not Secure' warning in every browser, and it says you started in both 1982 and 1988. Customers notice that stuff. Here's a modern version with everything you already offer, organized so people call."

They have a real site, so expect "we already have one." Answer by showing both on a phone, side by side.

## Confirm before launch
- [ ] Founding year (1982 or 1988) and the correct address (Grimes Ln or Walnut St)
- [ ] Which phone to feature ((812) 332-0711 is the main number per most listings)
- [ ] Domain access, since we'll point harrisheatingandair.com to the new site and add HTTPS
- [ ] Whether air duct cleaning is still offered (Yellow Pages lists it, but the demo doesn't)

## Outreach
