**Recommended offer:** Standard $900 + Care Plan $39/mo

## Why they're the #1 prospect
- **No website.** Their online presence is a Facebook page (199 likes), a Nextdoor page and two *unclaimed* Yelp listings.
- **Their listings contradict each other.** There are two addresses (6352 W Cowden Rd and 200 E Association St Ste 206A), three phone numbers ((812) 876-1097, (812) 908-5155 and (812) 908-5151), and two sets of hours (8 to 5 vs 9 to 5). Customers can't tell which number to call, and Google trusts consistent listings more.
- **They have social proof they aren't using.** They were a **Nextdoor Neighborhood Favorite 2024** and show 100% recommend on Facebook.
- **They're established and can pay.** Directories say they've been in business 43 to 45 years and serve both homes and businesses.

## Pitch angle
> "You're a Neighborhood Favorite, but when someone in Ellettsville searches 'furnace repair' this month, there's no Durham website, and there are three different phone numbers for you floating around online. I built you a site and I can clean up those listings."

The Standard tier's listing cleanup matters more here than for any other lead. Lead with it.

## Confirm before launch (ask on the first call)
- [ ] Correct phone, address (Cowden Rd or Association St?) and hours
- [ ] Years in business. Directories say since about 1981. Only claim what the owner confirms.
- [ ] The owner's name. A third-party database says Charles Durham, but that's unverified, so ask.
- [ ] Towns served. The demo assumes Ellettsville, Bloomington, Stinesville, Spencer and Gosport.
- [ ] Email address for form leads, plus photos of the team or vans (with permission)
