**Live demo:** https://sfkone24.github.io/Website-Creation/jones-plumbing-excavating/
**Recommended offer:** Standard $900 (listings cleanup is the main value here)
**Status:** demo built. Lowest priority of this batch because of mixed reviews and conflicting records.

## Why they're a prospect
- **No website.** jonesplumbingin.com is a Logansport, IN company, and jonesplumbing.net is an Illinois company. Nextdoor even shows the Illinois company's email on their listing.
- **Their records are a mess:** three entity names (Jones Plumbing Inc, Jones Plumbing and Excavating LLC, Jones Plumbing & Excavating), two addresses (1965 N US Hwy 231 vs 4160 Freedom Rd), three sets of hours, and founding year 2004 vs "30 years."
- **Plumbing and excavation in one company** is a real selling point for rural Owen County.
- **Watch out:** reviews are mixed (Angi 5.0 from 2013–16, Birdeye 3.4, and a 2019 one-star septic complaint). Don't bring up reviews.

## Pitch angle
> "Your listings show three company names and two addresses, and Nextdoor shows an email for a plumber in Illinois. A site of your own puts the right name, number and services in one place."
