## Why they're a strong prospect
- **No website found.** A business directory (D&B) lists terrellheatingac.com, but search engines have no pages indexed for it. Their presence is Facebook, Nextdoor, BBB and directories.
- **They have a 40-year story that isn't online:** *"Family owned and operated since 1984"* (from their Facebook page) and an **A+ BBB rating**.
- **Their directory data is a mess.** Listings show three addresses (5440 N Union Valley Rd, 3500 W Maple Grove Rd and 8477 N Sarada Dr in Ellettsville), and one Yellow Pages listing says "1025 years in business."
- **They specialize in Ducane and Tempstar**, so a site can rank for "Tempstar dealer Bloomington" searches.

## Pitch angle
